How to use it
  1. Use Chrome or Edge. Press Start listening and allow the microphone. Place the device near the speaker, or connect it to the sound desk.
  2. Choose who is speaking now: English (pastor), Deutsch (translator) or Twi (typed notes, see below). The language button decides what the computer listens for.
  3. When the pastor names a scripture, a card appears on the right. Press P (or click Project) to show it on the projector, C to clear the screen.
  4. In "Projector settings" you can change this: Ask me is the button/key behaviour above; Automatic shows the verse by itself after 3 seconds unless you press Cancel; Voice trigger shows it when the pastor says a phrase you choose (e.g. "project it"). You can also choose English only, German only, or both languages side by side on the projector.
  5. Click Open projector window, drag that window to the projector screen, and press F in it for full screen.
  6. You can correct any text by clicking on it. Everything saves automatically in this browser. Export to Word, text or PDF when the service ends.

Twi: browsers cannot transcribe Twi automatically. In Twi mode, type short notes in the box under the transcript (press Enter). Bible references you type or say in English or German are still detected.
