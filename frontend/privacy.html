Privacy notice

This page explains, in plain language, what information Sermon Scribe keeps about you and why.

What we store

  • Your name and email address, so you can sign in and so we know whose account is whose.
  • A securely scrambled version of your password (never the password itself - we cannot read it, even if we wanted to).
  • When you signed up, when you last logged in, and how many times you have logged in, plus which browser/device type you used (your "user agent") - never your IP address or location.
  • The sermons, notes, and settings you create in your own account.

Why we store it

Only to make the app work for you: to let you log in, to keep your sermons safe and private to you, and to let an administrator keep the list of accounts healthy (for example, to see how many people are using the app, or to disable an account if needed).

Who can see it

Nobody but you can see your sermons or notes - not other users, not even an administrator. An administrator can only see your name, email, sign-up date, login activity, how many sermons you have, and how much you've used the AI features below (requests and characters, as counts only, never the content).

We never sell or share your information with anyone outside of running this app.

AI correction and translation

This feature is off by default. If you turn it on (in the app, under "My account"), the text of a paragraph is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, only at the moment it's finished, only to fix spelling/grammar and, if you ask for it, to translate it - never for any other purpose, and never sold. Your original words are always kept unchanged alongside whatever the AI returns, and you can turn this off again at any time.

An administrator can also turn the AI features off for everyone at once, and can see how many AI requests and characters each account has used (never the text itself, as above).

How to delete your data

You are always in control. Inside the app, under "My account", you can:

  • Download my data - get a copy of everything stored about you, as a file.
  • Delete my account - this permanently and immediately erases your account and every sermon you have saved. It cannot be undone.
